## Relations and construction

Collinearity is tested between actual exposed vertical boundary faces across
an already selected stacked contact. A close left or right edge supplies an
affine offset error and a candidate shared supporting line. A one-sided capture
retains the upper frame's intrinsic width rather than collapsing it or silently
resizing both edges. Source geometry, deliberate insets and contained-opening
margins constrain admission. Equal coordinates do not fuse source-site identity
or require the segments to become a single stroke.

Outward symmetry requires a front-like view, one upper frame and distinct
source-supported reflected motifs in its parent. A roof and cross inheriting
one upper axis do not supply additional votes for that axis. Repeated routes
from the same acquired support are counted once. The accepted upper frame can
consume its parent's midpoint; its already attached roof and cross then follow
that relation rather than receiving separate position assignments.

Both use existing native attraction, admission, capture, response capacity and
release-pressure mechanisms. The support surfaces now retain actual supporting
construction references alongside the operative exposed ports. These references
explain admission; they are not extra response routes or duplicated evidence.
Parameter changes enter through native tension. Exact holding comes from the
accepted affine construction, not display rounding or a global solver.

## Cost and communication direction

The complete church run took 579.31 seconds, including native history creation,
archive production/restore and review generation. Its compressed worlds total
1,243,596,323 bytes. These measurements do not isolate settlement arithmetic.
The [previous timing breakdown](church-runtime-2026-10-29.json) separates local
provider calls, the wider computation and archive work.

The native control's sixteen rounds took about 1.55 seconds, including a second
baseline scene, saved projections and native witness creation. It is a much
smaller workload and excludes final encoding/restore from that timer. It is
not a church-throughput estimate. Native inputs remove sketch conversion;
retaining a live structure can also remove repeated scene import. Evaluation,
routing, local response, competition and structure changes still require work.
A category word alone has no transforming power without connected priors and
construction candidates.

The communication direction is to separate local competence from contribution
to connected neighbours. An arithmetic relation can be correct and unhelpful.
Existing response witnesses expose before/after effects; estimating utility
needs repeated occasions, shared-source provenance and comparisons that avoid
rewarding a self-confirming explanation. Facilitators and filters can specialize
through exposed routes, calibration, admissibility and capacity, while retaining
blocked participation. This milestone does not train that utility or introduce
general adaptive routing. An exclusion structure is also distinct from the
kernel's formal dual and from conserved material energy.
