## Diagnosis

The earlier rules allowed small attachment gaps. Outside the allowed intervals,
damping, quarter-unit response rounding, finite passage capacity and persistent
source/seed opinions could leave a compromise. Continuing the same soft
negotiation does not guarantee exact symmetry or contact.

The existing wall partition excluded towers from wall cells but did not remove
neighboring tower overlap. Window pairing also rejected provisional
round-versus-angular pairs before considering whether repetition should revise
one member's family. In sample 6, a floating outline had become a second door
through its floor-reaching envelope; an incomplete reflected counterpart was
rejected. The eight-window initial proposal safety limit was not the cause.

## New structures

Broad attraction remains. Starting at pass three, eligible nearby relationships
can substitute executable shared affine constructions: aligned roof/support
bounds, centered cross frames, upper tower/wall bases, neighboring facade side
boundaries, common floors and reflected opening frames. The compound machine
exposes actual native part-bound ports and calibrated private response families.
Parameter changes preserve a captured relation exactly.

The controller records capture/hold/release state and native distance/pressure
claims. Strong opposing uncoupled requests can reopen a relationship on a later
pass; release imports the current public geometry without an immediate jump.
The previous construction, rounding remainder, port correspondence and actual
replacement displacement remain readable. These are authored diagnostic
thresholds, not a physical force law or learned energy landscape.

Acquired source sites are not fused just because their projected coordinates
agree. A geometric bound binding and a source-graph joint replacement remain
distinct operations.

Multiple opening rows can be reconsidered. A grounded main entrance, floating
source ink and a supported reflected counterpart can challenge an earlier door
role or admit an incomplete window. A common family competes with independent
family costs. Substantial upper corners supply a rectangular opinion; tiny
hooks and curved controls do not count as those corners. The earlier role,
family, source intervals and competing costs remain recorded.

## What remains unresolved

The four open cross proposals, other nonzero error reads and unresolved ink stay
visible. A held frame does not prove correct object identity or interpretation.
Family/role inference still occurs at admission; pressure does not yet trigger
general rediscovery of windows, parent roles or alternative head families.
Mirroring binds frames, while interior details can retain separate constructions.
The collision rule concerns admitted neighboring facade columns, not every
legitimate nesting or projected overlap.

Source mismatch can rise while conceptual structure improves. For example,
sample 6's unexplained source-length fraction goes from about 18.5% at admission
to 25.6% at pass eight; sample 11 goes from 5.6% to 9.6%. These are geometric
coverage diagnostics, not recognition scores. Unknown intervals remain available.

Complete native histories are bulky: the final 16 archives total 1,242,040,901
compressed bytes. This remains a bounded experiment; trace storage and execution
cost should be addressed before a production-scale run of this extension.
